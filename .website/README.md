# UNxR - How to Play

> Sound note: the size limit kept me from adding music in the quality and amount I planned (as if that is not why we're all here :)). Only a tuning fork remains; call this a demo. The post-compo build will feature a track by awesome Katie Spogreeva, @Spogr.

## Mission

UNxR (pronounced “UNK-ser”) is a neon rhythm shooter. Ride the tunnel edge, line up with enemies, and clear each wave. The gun fires by itself. Survive 16 arena shapes and chase a high score.

## Controls

**Keyboard:** `Left` / `A` and `Right` / `D` move. `Shift` / `X` uses Rainbow Flush. `P` / `Esc` pauses. `M` toggles sound.

**Touch:** tap either side to move. Double-tap for Rainbow Flush. Swipe and tap in menus. Play in portrait mode.

## Stay on the beat

Move when an arrow reaches the diamond. Hit the beat and you move; miss and your gun skips a shot.

Closed arenas wrap around. Open arenas have real ends.

## Score

| Target | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Flipper | 100 |
| Tanker | 140 |
| Spiker | 180 |
| Pulsar | 200 |
| Fuseball | 250 / 500 / 750 |
| Spike hit | 2 |

Fuseballs are worth more up close. Every 20,000 points gives an extra life, up to six. You start with three.

A later start pays its bonus after you clear that level.

## Special rules

- A Tanker splits into two enemies.
- A Spiker builds shootable spikes.
- Fuseball: a star from level 11. It races on rails and is vulnerable only while jumping.
- Pulsar: an electric wave from level 17. It warns, then charges its whole lane. Move!
- Your shots can destroy enemy shots.
- Spikes stay deadly between levels, but your gun keeps firing.

Each wave has two Rainbow Flushes. The first clears the arena; the second kills one enemy. Waiting enemies survive. Charges return next wave, not after a lost life.
